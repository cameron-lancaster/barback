Barback Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Barback is a shared beer and wine catalogue for bar staff, with tasting notes from the whole team. We collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and there are no ads or tracking.

Who we are

Barback is made by Cameron Lancaster. Questions about this policy or your data: cameronlancaster@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your Apple sign-in. You sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an account identifier and, on your first sign-in only, your name and email address (or a private relay address) if you choose to share them.
  • Your profile. Your name, email, and, if you add them, your phone number and profile photo. If you tap "Use My Contact Card", the app fills these in from the one contact card you pick; it doesn't read the rest of your contacts.
  • Your business. The business name, address, logo, and invite code, and who belongs to it.
  • The catalogue. Products your team adds: names, styles, grapes or hops, vintage, ABV, prices, tags, label photos, and tasting notes, each with who wrote it and when.
  • Label scans. When you scan a label, the photos you take and the text your phone reads from them. We keep a log of each scan's text and results to measure and improve scan quality.

The camera is used only while you're scanning a label or taking a product photo. We don't collect your location or browsing history, we don't use advertising identifiers, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

Only to run the app: showing your business's catalogue and tasting notes to you and your coworkers, reading labels you scan, and writing product overviews. Your name and photo are shown to people in your business, for example on the tasting notes you write.

AI features

Barback uses Anthropic's Claude to read label photos and to write AI Overviews and suggestions:

  • Scanning sends the label photos you take and the text your phone read from them.
  • AI Overviews and suggestions send a product's details: type, producer, name, style, grapes or hops, vintage, and ABV.

We don't send your name, email, phone, or tasting notes to Anthropic. Anthropic processes this data to return results to the app and, under its commercial terms, doesn't use it to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Who can see it

  • People in your business see the catalogue, tasting notes, business details, and each member's name, photo, email, and phone.
  • Everyone else sees nothing. Joining a business needs its invite code, and anyone in the business can generate a new one.
  • Service providers. Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and photos. Sign-in is handled by Apple, and AI features by Anthropic (above).

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Keeping it and deleting it

We keep your data while you have an account. A business's catalogue belongs to the business: if you leave or are removed, the products and tasting notes you added stay with it.

You can delete your account anytime in the app under Profile → Delete Account. When you do, we:

  • delete your profile photo, phone, and email, and remove you from your business;
  • delete your sign-in and revoke Barback's access to your Apple ID;
  • show "Former member" instead of your name on tasting notes you wrote, so your team keeps its notes;
  • if you're the last member of your business, delete the business and its whole catalogue.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Our database only lets people read and change their own business's data, photos are stored privately, and only the author of a tasting note can edit it.

Who it's for

Barback is for adults who work at bars, restaurants, and shops that serve alcohol. It isn't meant for anyone under the legal drinking age, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
